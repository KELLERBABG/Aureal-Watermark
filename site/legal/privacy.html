Privacy Policy (Datenschutzerklärung)

1. General Principles & Zero-Data Guarantee

Aureal Watermark is engineered for strict confidentiality and audio data sovereignty. The audio-processing features do not collect, transmit, profile, or store your audio recordings or usage telemetry. License validation is a separate optional network request to Polar.sh when you enter a commercial license key.

  • Client-Side Audio Processing: All watermark embedding, frequency analysis, and matched-filter verification occur 100% locally in the memory of your browser or local device. No audio samples ever leave your machine.
  • No Outbound Telemetry: Neither the CLI (`aureal-watermark.cjs`), the standalone executable (`aureal-watermark.exe`), nor the Web Studio (`/studio`) make network requests, phone-home checks, or analytics calls.
  • No Tracking Cookies: This website does not use tracking cookies, advertising pixels, or third-party analytics scripts.
