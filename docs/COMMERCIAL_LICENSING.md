## Regulatory Compliance: The EU AI Act & C2PA Bridge

### 1. EU AI Act Article 50 Machine-Readable Compliance
Under **Article 50 of the European Union AI Act**, providers of generative AI systems must ensure that AI-generated audio and deepfake synthetic voices are marked in an unalterable, machine-detectable format.
* **The Vulnerability of Metadata:** Traditional metadata (ID3 tags, WAV RIFF chunks, C2PA manifest envelopes) is routinely stripped when audio is uploaded to TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts, WhatsApp, or compressed via lossy codecs.
* **The Aureal Solution:** Aureal embeds the provenance identifier directly inside the acoustic wave itself using Direct-Sequence Spread Spectrum (DSSS) modulation. It survives MP3/AAC compression, volume changes, and format conversion without audible degradation.

### 2. The C2PA Content Credentials Persistence Bridge
Standard C2PA manifests contain cryptographic signatures proving content origin. Aureal’s 32-bit payload ID functions as a persistent **Acoustic Pointer** that survives where metadata fails:
$$\text{Social Media Video Upload} \longrightarrow \text{Metadata Stripped} \longrightarrow \text{Aureal Acoustic Scan} \longrightarrow \text{Original C2PA Manifest Recovered}$$
