1. General Principles & Zero-Data Guarantee

Aureal Watermark is engineered from the ground up for strict confidentiality and data sovereignty. The software does not collect, transmit, profile, or store any personal data, usage telemetry, or audio recordings.

  • Client-Side Audio Processing: All watermark embedding, frequency analysis, and matched-filter verification occur 100% locally in the memory of your browser or local device. No audio samples ever leave your machine.
  • No Outbound Telemetry: Neither the CLI (`aureal-watermark.cjs`), the standalone executable (`aureal-watermark.exe`), nor the Web Studio (`studio.html`) make network requests, phone-home checks, or analytics calls.
  • No Tracking Cookies: This website does not use tracking cookies, advertising pixels, or third-party analytics scripts.
