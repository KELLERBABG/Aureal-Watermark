Frequently Asked Questions

What counts as free non-commercial use?

Personal evaluation, student academic research, non-monetized tracks, and bedroom listening tests are 100% free under the PolyForm Noncommercial 1.0.0 license. No payment required.

Does Aureal require internet or phone-home DRM?

No. Aureal is designed for high-security, air-gapped studio facilities. It runs completely offline on your own machine without telemetry, accounts, or DRM phone-home servers.

How does the Promo Leak Audit tool work?

You run batch_distribute.js to generate tagged copies for reviewers and DJs. If a leaked copy surfaces on Discord, YouTube, or SoundCloud, you run audit_leak.js to extract the ID and identify the recipient in seconds.

Can I pay via corporate invoice or wire transfer?

Yes. We support corporate purchase orders (PO), SEPA / ACH bank wire transfers, and credit card payments. Digital license certificates and tax invoices are issued same-day.
