# Action Plan: Launching Commercial Software Licensing (Side Business)

This step-by-step operational plan outlines the legal, financial, and technical workflow for transitioning your local audio-watermarking project into a commercial dual-license model while completing vocational training in Germany.

---

## Overview & Architecture

```
[Customer / Studio]
        │  Buys License ($249 - $12.5k)
        ▼
[Merchant of Record: Polar.sh / Lemon Squeezy]
   ├── Collects payment (CC / Wire / PayPal)
   ├── Handles international B2B sales tax & EU VAT
   ├── Delivers binary / license key / repo access
   └── Generates compliant invoice for buyer
        │
        │  Single monthly consolidation payout
        ▼
[Dedicated Business Bank Account (e.g. N26 Business / Finom)]
        │
        ▼
[Finanzamt / Einnahmen-Überschuss-Rechnung (EÜR)]
```

## Phase 2: Business & Tax Registration

### 2.1 Trade Registration (*Gewerbeanmeldung*)
* [x] **Action guide prepared:** Documented in [`docs/GEWERBE_UND_STEUER_LEITFADEN.md`](docs/GEWERBE_UND_STEUER_LEITFADEN.md).
* [ ] **Submit online:** Register through the Wirtschafts-Service-Portal.NRW (`service.wirtschaft.nrw`) or local Gewerbeamt.
  * **Field designation:** *Nebenerwerb* (part-time / secondary commercial activity).
  * **Activity description:**  
    > *„Entwicklung und digitaler Vertrieb von Standardsoftware und Softwarelizenzen im Nebenerwerb sowie damit verbundene Beratungs- und Supportdienstleistungen.“*
  * **Registration fee:** Approx. €20 – €40 (one-time).

### 2.2 Dedicated Bank Account Setup
* [x] **Account separation protocol:** Documented in [`docs/GEWERBE_UND_STEUER_LEITFADEN.md`](docs/GEWERBE_UND_STEUER_LEITFADEN.md).
* [ ] **Open a zero-cost digital business account:**
  * Open a free account (e.g., **N26 Business Standard** or **Finom Solo**).
  * Receive a dedicated German DE-IBAN exclusively for receiving payouts and paying for repo infrastructure/domains.
  * Keep personal Sparkasse account 100% isolated from commercial transactions.

### 2.3 Tax Questionnaire (*Fragebogen zur steuerlichen Erfassung*)
* [x] **Guidance and decision matrix prepared:** Documented in [`docs/GEWERBE_UND_STEUER_LEITFADEN.md`](docs/GEWERBE_UND_STEUER_LEITFADEN.md).
* [ ] Once the Gewerbeamt registers your data, log into **ELSTER** to complete the questionnaire sent by the *Finanzamt*.
* [ ] **VAT regime choice (*Kleinunternehmerregelung* vs. Standard):**
  * *Recommended: § 19 UStG (Kleinunternehmer)* for Year 1 (under €25,000 threshold). Merchant of Record handles all international and EU VAT.
